# Conceptual Design and Multi-Fidelity Aerodynamic Assessment of a Hybrid VTOL UAV

**Degree:** M.S. by Research  
**Department:** Aerospace Engineering, IIT Kanpur  
**Project Type:** M.S. Thesis  
**Duration:** May 2025 – Present  
**Supervisor:** Dr. Abhishek, Department of Aerospace Engineering, IIT Kanpur

---

## Overview

This research focuses on the conceptual design, aerodynamic assessment, propulsion sizing, prototype development, and experimental evaluation of a hybrid VTOL UAV combining multirotor-based hover capability with wing-borne flight during cruise.

The objective is to investigate a lifting-wing UAV configuration capable of operating efficiently across hover, transition, and forward-flight regimes while integrating aerodynamic analysis, propulsion modelling, structural considerations, and experimental validation.

The research follows a multi-fidelity engineering workflow, progressing from conceptual sizing and low-fidelity aerodynamic analysis to higher-fidelity CFD, prototype development, and flight testing.

---

## Research Objectives

The primary objectives of the thesis are:

- Develop a hybrid lifting-wing VTOL UAV configuration.
- Perform mission-based aircraft sizing and component-level weight estimation.
- Design the lifting wing using aerodynamic and geometric constraints.
- Evaluate low-Reynolds-number aerodynamic characteristics.
- Develop a multi-fidelity aerodynamic analysis framework.
- Estimate hover propulsion requirements using Blade Element Momentum Theory.
- Assess aerodynamic performance and longitudinal stability.
- Develop and fabricate a full-scale UAV prototype.
- Conduct experimental aerodynamic and flight testing.
- Compare computational predictions with experimental observations.
